## Requirements

- [ ] A **new** Lens for Snap Spectacles, created during the submission period (this project was created on 2026-11-18).
- [ ] Planned with the Devpost Learn skill pack. Planning documents are in `devpost/`.
- [ ] Built through the CLAD plugin, Lens Studio and the Lens Studio MCP.
- [ ] Runs in the Lens Studio preview. No SPECS device is needed.
- [ ] **Demo video**: under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, includes a screen recording of the Lens running in the Lens Studio preview, no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music without permission.
- [ ] **Repository** on GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket containing the Lens Studio project files and the `devpost/` folder at the root. A private repository must be on GitHub or GitLab and shared with the two Devpost reviewer addresses listed in the official rules before the deadline.
- [ ] **Text description**: what the Lens does, who it is for and what was learned.
- [ ] All submission materials in English.
- [ ] Any pre-existing code or work used is disclosed (templates, packages and AI assistants are allowed).

Reviewers are not required to clone or run the project, so the video has to show it working.

## Judging criteria (equal weight; ties are broken in this order)

1. **Design**: a complete, coherent product experience, not just a technical proof of concept.
2. **Technical Implementation**: how well it is built and how well it uses CLAD, Lens Studio and the Lens Studio MCP.
3. **Innovation/Idea**: how creative and novel the concept is.
4. **Presentation**: does the video show it working end to end, and does the pitch say what problem is solved, who it is for and why it matters.
